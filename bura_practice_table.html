How to play

Lead 1–5 cards of one suit. The answer must use the same number of cards; it wins only if every led card is beaten (higher card of the same suit, or any trump on a side suit). You may always give cards face down instead.

Card order: 6 7 8 9 J Q K 10 A. Points: A 11, 10 10, K 4, Q 3, J 2.

After winning a trick you may claim ვარ if your pile has 61+. A wrong claim loses the round. Raise დავი on your turn; გატანება gives up the round.
